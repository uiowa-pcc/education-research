Research the School & District

You do not need to know everything. Find a few details that help you understand the role, explain why you are interested, and ask thoughtful questions.

Step 1 of 3

Start with the basics

Step 2 of 3

Find only what you can use

1. Priorities

Look at the district/school website, strategic plan, principal message, job posting, or recent news.

2. Students & community

Notice the community the school serves, programs offered, grade levels, student supports, and anything that helps you understand context. Avoid making assumptions from demographics alone.

3. Something specific

Find one program, initiative, course, school tradition, instructional approach, or recent development that genuinely interests you.

4. Your connection

Step 3 of 3

Your research notes

Use the connection—not the facts alone. A stronger “Why this school?” answer explains what interested you and why it fits what you hope to contribute.
